## Documentation & Process Design

Throughout my career, I’ve gravitated toward the kind of work that makes future work easier: writing things down, clarifying the messy, and making processes repeatable without handholding. Whether it’s a runbook, a Confluence space, or a crisis protocol, I build documentation that’s actually usable.

### Strengths & Habits
- **Confluence Authoring**: Created and curated team spaces that centralized vendor info, procedural guides, and tribal knowledge.
- **Runbooks & SOPs**: Developed operational documentation for systems like Atlassian, BladeLogic, WebSphere, and custom pipelines.
- **Crisis Protocols**: Wrote structured emergency response guides to reduce confusion and restore service faster during incidents.
- **Training Resources**: Documented technical systems and processes for non-technical staff, increasing adoption and reducing dependency.

### Projects & Examples
- Built the internal documentation strategy for Grinnell’s Automate Plus transition.
- Developed onboarding guides and playbooks for Atlassian-managed service clients.
- Translated complex middleware automation steps into readable, repeatable instructions.

### Philosophy
Good documentation isn’t about filling pages. It’s about removing uncertainty. My goal is always the same: make the next person’s job easier—whether that person is me next week or someone I’ll never meet.
