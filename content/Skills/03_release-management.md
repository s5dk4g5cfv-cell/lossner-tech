## Release Management

Before “DevOps” was a buzzword, I was already immersed in the daily realities of coordinating, documenting, and executing software releases at scale. My approach to release management is pragmatic—rooted in process discipline, clear communication, and automation wherever possible.

### Areas of Focus
- **CI/CD Pipelines**: Designed and maintained automated pipelines using Jenkins, Bamboo, and Automate. Supported deployments across dev, test, QA, and production environments.
- **Configuration Management**: Managed proprietary and vendor configuration items (CIs) using SCCM principles. Audited, packaged, and released code with traceability in mind.
- **Change Control**: Administered tools like StarTeam and RIMS, ensuring structured approvals and visibility across over 30 corporate clients.
- **Emergency Fixes & Runbooks**: Documented repeatable emergency deployment paths and rollback procedures. Reduced tribal knowledge dependency through clear protocols.
- **Cross-Team Coordination**: Worked closely with developers, QA, infrastructure, and business teams to ensure clean handoffs and clear expectations during release windows.

### Philosophy
A good release process reduces friction, not freedom. It’s about flow—keeping changes moving without letting chaos through the door. My goal is always the same: fast, clean, recoverable deployments that build trust across teams.
