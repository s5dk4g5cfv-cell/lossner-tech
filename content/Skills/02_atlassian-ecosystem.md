## Atlassian Ecosystem

I've worked deeply with Atlassian tools for over a decade—supporting, extending, and migrating them across enterprise environments. My experience isn’t limited to user-level administration; I’ve developed plugins, integrated them with other systems, and led major migrations that required careful planning and execution.

### Tools & Responsibilities
- **Jira (ACP-620 Certified)**: Managed projects, workflows, and custom field configurations for internal teams and clients. Led Jira Server → Data Center transitions. Provided training and documentation.
- **Confluence**: Built and maintained structured spaces for vendor documentation, runbooks, and SOPs. Encouraged teams to document tribal knowledge and collaborate transparently.
- **Bitbucket**: Guided teams on branch management and source control practices. Supported migrations from legacy tools (e.g., Subversion) to Bitbucket.
- **Bamboo**: Integrated Bamboo with ServiceNow and deployment systems. Wrote custom scripts to automate build/deploy chains.
- **Crowd**: Administered during enterprise-wide migrations. Focused on user management and directory integration.

### Projects & Milestones
- Led full-stack Atlassian Server → Data Center migration for Jira, Confluence, Bitbucket, and Crowd.
- Developed internal plugins bridging Jira/Bamboo with ServiceNow to improve workflow integration.
- Participated in Atlassian Cloud Migration simulations and completed official migration training.

### Philosophy
I treat Atlassian tools as collaborative infrastructure. When they’re configured with intention, they fade into the background and let teams focus on meaningful work. When neglected, they become noise and friction.

I aim for the former.
