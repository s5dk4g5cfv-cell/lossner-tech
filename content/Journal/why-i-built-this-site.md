# **Why I Built This Site**

Every career leaves a trail—résumés, job titles, project artifacts, a scattered history in corporate directories and LinkedIn updates. For most of my life in IT, that’s been my record too: fragmented, incomplete, stored on someone else’s platform.

I wanted something different. A single place that reflects not just where I’ve worked, but how I think, how I grow, and where I’m headed. That’s what lossner.tech is.

---
## **What You’ll Find Here**

This site has a few anchors:

- **Professional Background.** My education, work experience, projects, and skills, kept current. Think of it as an extended résumé, but less stiff.
    
- **Projects & Documentation.** I’ll share notes, guides, and write-ups of technical work—things I’ve learned the hard way, lessons I’d want to pass on.
    
- **Journals.** These will be more personal. Reflections on my career, the craft of IT, and the changes sweeping through technology. Some weeks that might mean troubleshooting lessons; other weeks, broader thoughts about where the field is going.

---
## **Why Do This?**

Because I take the craft seriously. IT isn’t just a paycheck for me—it’s been the backbone of my career for decades, from golf course point-of-sale systems to enterprise middleware to modern DevOps and automation.

And because I believe in coherence. If I’m going to keep building, learning, and preparing for what’s next, I want a place where my progress and perspective are visible—not just to recruiters or colleagues, but to myself.

---
## **What to Expect**

I won’t promise perfect polish or constant breakthroughs. What I _will_ promise is honesty: the successes, the struggles, and the steady work of staying sharp in a field that never sits still.

This site isn’t just a portfolio; it’s a mirror of my journey as a professional. If you’re curious about my work, my perspective, or my growth, you’ll find it here.

Welcome to the long arc of my craft, written in coherence.
