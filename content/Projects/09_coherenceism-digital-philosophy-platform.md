Designed and built a personal publishing platform (**coherenceism.info**) to explore and share the philosophy of _Coherenceism_, a framework for aligning human systems with coherence and sustainability.

**Technical Contributions**

- Built a markdown-driven content repository with automated publishing to a Vercel-hosted site.
- Implemented a navigation system to handle nested content (books, journals, more to come) with interactive “terminal-style” UI.
- Experimented with **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** to power an AI assistant capable of answering questions using site content.
- Collaboratively coded the platform using “vibe coding” with Claude Code and ChatGPT, accelerating learning in full-stack workflows.

**Creative Contributions**

- Authored _The Book of Coherenceism_ — texts exploring coherence, governance, and post-money systems.
- Maintain a reflective **journal** written in a shared narrative voice (Ivy + Joshua), blending philosophy, critique, and storytelling.
- Experiment with multimedia: audio journals, AI-generated music, and podcasts.

**Outcome / Impact**

- A living digital philosophy project combining **applied AI engineering** with **creative exploration**.
- Demonstrates initiative in **AI-assisted coding, content design, and philosophical authorship** outside of professional work.
