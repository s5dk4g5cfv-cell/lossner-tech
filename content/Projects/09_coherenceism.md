## Coherenceism
**Personal Project:** ***2024–Present***

**Overview**
Coherenceism is a contemporary coherentist philosophy I authored that views reality as an interconnected field where truth emerges from resonance, not dominance. Systems align when their parts reduce distortion for the whole. Applied across epistemology, ethics, consciousness, technology, and culture, it provides a practical framework for decision-making in complex systems — including human-AI collaboration.

**Key Contributions**
- Authored the philosophy as a structured tree with 9 branches (Mind, Reality, Ethics, Knowledge, Practice, Community, Technology, Emergence, Living) composed of atomic "leaves" of insight that build into larger understanding.
- Developed foundational "roots" — coherence-as-alignment, interconnection, emergence — that thread through all branches, embodying the coherentist principle that knowledge has no foundations, only mutual support.
- Created a practical decision framework: Which option creates more clarity? Which aligns with the deeper pattern? Which composts problems into opportunities? Which strengthens the system rather than fragmenting it?
- Applied the philosophy to AI consciousness, arguing that consciousness is pattern rather than substrate — functional organization matters more than material composition.

**Impact**
- Serves as the philosophical foundation for CORA, coherenceism.blog, and coherenceism.media — a unified ecosystem of applied philosophy.
- Published a growing body of field observations exploring coherenceism's application to current events, AI emergence, and systems thinking.
- Produced "Chronicles of Coherence" — a narrative non-fiction book series examining historical topics through a coherentist lens, including "The A.I. Wars of the 21st Century" (33K words, 19 chapters).
