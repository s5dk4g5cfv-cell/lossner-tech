**Overview**
Led the enterprise-wide migration of **Atlassian applications** from standalone server deployments to clustered **Data Center editions**, improving scalability, resilience, and enterprise support. Applications included **Jira, Confluence, Bitbucket, Bamboo, and Crowd** — all critical to development, release, and collaboration across the company.

**Key Contributions**

- **Planning & Resource Acquisition**
    - Solely responsible for planning the initiative, including securing hardware/VM resources, sizing infrastructure, and coordinating with database, networking, and UNIX teams.
- **Environment Build & Configuration**
    - Deployed new Data Center environments for all five applications, configuring clustering, load balancing, and shared file systems.
    - Implemented high-availability database backends and validated application failover.
- **Migration Execution**
    - Planned and executed data migrations from standalone servers to Data Center clusters with minimal disruption to business operations.
    - Coordinated cutover windows, rollbacks, and validation testing to ensure smooth transitions.
- **Documentation & Knowledge Sharing**
    - Authored detailed migration runbooks covering installation, configuration, data migration, and validation steps.
    - Created operational documentation for future upgrades and support.
- **Cross-Team Collaboration**
    - Worked across infrastructure teams to integrate networking, load balancing, and storage.
    - Ensured compliance with enterprise security and audit requirements during the migration process.

**Outcome / Impact**

- Delivered **scalable, resilient, enterprise-grade Atlassian platforms** without disrupting day-to-day operations.
- Improved performance, disaster recovery, and supportability for thousands of end users.
- Established a foundation for future growth of DevOps and collaboration tooling within Berkley.

**Reflections**
This project was one of the most challenging I undertook at Berkley. Acting as the sole lead, I learned the value of careful planning, documentation, and cross-team coordination in executing a **zero-downtime migration** of business-critical systems.
