## Inventory Management Systems Modernization
**Kinley's Golf & Sports Center:** ***2004 - 2005***

**Overview**
Tasked with modernizing a manual, paper-based inventory system, I researched, designed, and implemented a fully electronic solution for Kinley’s Golf. As the sole technical employee, I independently drove vendor selection, system design, and rollout while balancing my academic studies.

**Key Contributions**

- Researched and selected **Microsoft Retail Management System**, partnering with Electronic Business Systems of Iowa for acquisition and deployment.
- Designed and built the IT infrastructure, including a dedicated server with SQL Server, networked point-of-sale (POS) terminals running Windows XP, and wireless connectivity.
- Led the **data migration effort**, scanning thousands of inventory items into a new database, including handling non-barcoded services like golf club repair and bowling operations.
- Authored business process documentation and created procedures for handling sales, repairs, and inventory tracking.
- Conducted **staff training** sessions for a dozen employees, many of whom had limited computer experience, ensuring smooth adoption of the new system.

**Outcome / Impact**
The project successfully transitioned Kinley’s Golf from manual inventory to a fully electronic system, improving accuracy, efficiency, and customer service. The successful deployment impressed company leadership, prompting the creation of a dedicated office space for my IT work.

**Reflections**
This project was my first full-cycle IT implementation — from research and vendor negotiation to infrastructure build-out, data migration, and staff enablement. It cemented my interest in blending technology with business operations and gave me confidence to lead technical initiatives independently.
