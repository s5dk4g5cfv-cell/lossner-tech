**Overview**
Recognizing the rapid growth of online marketplaces, I launched Kinley’s Golf’s first **eBay store**, transforming the company’s ability to sell used and collectible golf equipment nationwide. The project bridged traditional retail operations with digital sales, creating a new revenue stream and expanding the company’s customer base beyond local shoppers.

**Key Contributions**

- Researched and implemented **eBay Blackthorne** software to streamline product listing and store management.
- Designed efficient processes for photographing, cataloging, and listing items — reducing listing time to just minutes after trade-ins or acquisitions.
- Authored procedures and trained staff on eBay listing workflows, ensuring consistency and speed.
- Personally managed online customer service: responding quickly to inquiries, processing payments, and coordinating shipping.
- Oversaw logistics to guarantee **same-day shipping**, providing customers in the U.S. and Canada with reliable, timely service.

**Outcome / Impact**

- Generated **several thousand dollars in weekly revenue** through online auctions and store sales.
- Successfully sold collectible items like Scotty Cameron Titleist putters, followed by large volumes of used club inventory.
- Online demand was so strong that the company had to moderate listings to avoid depleting local retail inventory, underscoring the project’s success.
- Expanded Kinley’s brand visibility beyond Des Moines to customers across North America.

**Reflections**
This project introduced me to **e-commerce operations at scale**, teaching the importance of balancing digital efficiency with brick-and-mortar priorities. It also sharpened my skills in customer communication, process optimization, and online logistics — areas that continue to influence how I think about technology and user experience.
