**Overview**
As part of Berkley’s enterprise software change management team, I contributed to a major upgrade of **StarTeam**, the company’s change and configuration management system. The upgrade introduced a new **web application interface**, enabling broader access for business and IT users.

**Key Contributions**

- Supported the migration to the latest StarTeam platform, ensuring stability and compliance during the transition.
- Modified and customized **change request forms** across a dozen application projects, tailoring them to the workflows of different business units.
- Coordinated with development teams to validate new forms and processes, ensuring usability and alignment with audit requirements.
- Helped communicate and train stakeholders on the new web-based interface, improving adoption and reducing confusion during rollout.

**Outcome / Impact**
- Enabled more efficient, accessible change control processes for 200+ users across the organization.
- Improved traceability and usability of change request forms, strengthening compliance with SOX audit requirements.
- Supported smoother collaboration between IT and business units through a modernized web interface.

**Reflections**
This project deepened my hands-on experience with **enterprise-scale release tools** and reinforced the importance of **user-centered process design** in change management. It was an early stepping stone toward my later focus on DevOps automation and release governance.
