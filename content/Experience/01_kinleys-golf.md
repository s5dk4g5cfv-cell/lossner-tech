## General Employee - Operations and IT Specialist
**Kinley's Golf & Sports Center:** ***Feb 1993 - Dec 2006***

Over a 13-year tenure, served in progressively advanced roles from general labor to technical and operational leadership. Played a pivotal role in transforming Kinley's business systems, technology stack, and customer service experience.

### Highlights

- **Operations & IT Modernization**
  - Re-engineered Kinley's inventory procedures from manual to electronic, including full deployment of electronic inventory and digital order processing systems.
  - Designed and maintained the company’s entire IT infrastructure as the sole technical employee: server, desktop systems, network backups, and security.
  - Developed custom business tools and reports using Microsoft Retail Management, XML, Crystal Reports, and Microsoft Publisher.
  - Authored business process documentation and trained staff, including those unfamiliar with computers, greatly improving adoption and team proficiency.

- **Online Sales Expansion**
  - Launched and managed Kinley’s eBay store, leading to a 40% increase in used golf club sales in 2006.
  - Handled listing creation, customer service, and fulfillment logistics.

- **Customer-Focused Retail Services**
  - Ran the golf club repair shop, including all services, inventory, and customer-facing interactions.
  - Introduced and provided custom golf club fittings, tailoring equipment to client specifications and improving customer satisfaction.

- **Early Work & Skill Development**
  - Started at age 14 performing maintenance and yard work, eventually taking on increasing responsibilities during high school and college.
  - Developed hands-on technical and customer service skills through continuous engagement and initiative.

This multifaceted experience laid a strong foundation in IT systems, business process improvement, retail operations, and customer engagement.
