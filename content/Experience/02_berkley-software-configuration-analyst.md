## Software Configuration Analyst
**Berkley Technology Services:** ***Jan 2007 - Sep 2011***

Played a key role in software configuration and change management (SCCM), helping implement and improve release processes across development and production environments for proprietary and vendor systems.

### Key Contributions

- **Release & Change Management**
  - Managed releases for configuration items (CIs) across QA and production environments.
  - Oversaw tracking, building, deployment, and auditing of releases, improving SCCM policy compliance and process consistency.
  
- **Stakeholder Communication & Process Education**
  - Acted as liaison between IT and business departments to advocate for effective configuration management.
  - Helped tailor SCCM implementations to the unique needs of individual business units.

- **Tools Administration & Compliance**
  - Administered the StarTeam Change Management system, managing over 200 user accounts.
  - Conducted regular SOX compliance audits to ensure regulatory adherence.
  - Maintained the Release Information Management System (RIMS), overseeing approvals for 30+ corporate clients.

- **Documentation & Process Design**
  - Created standard operating procedures (SOPs) for packaging, releasing, and communicating software changes.
  - Worked with cross-functional teams to ensure reliable emergency fix processes and structured release cycles.

This role strengthened my foundation in enterprise-scale release management and compliance-focused software lifecycle practices, preparing me for future DevOps and automation roles.
